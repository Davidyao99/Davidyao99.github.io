About Me

I am currently a Machine Learning Engineer in the Geometric Vision team at Tesla Autopilot. Previously, I completed my Master's in Computer Science with thesis at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where I was fortunate to work with my amazing mentor Prof. Shenlong Wang. My research focused on combining deep learning and classical 3D vision to design robust systems for multi-view synchronization and 4D modeling from monocular videos.

My interests lie broadly in 3D vision, neural rendering, deep learning and their applications in autonomous driving and robotics.
